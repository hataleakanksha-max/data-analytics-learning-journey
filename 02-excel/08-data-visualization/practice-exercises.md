# Lesson 9: Data Visualization — Practice Exercises

## Exercise 1: Identify the Chart Type

1. Compare sales across different products.
   - Answer: Bar or column chart.

2. Track revenue from January to December.
   - Answer: Line chart.

3. Show the share of total budget allocated to three departments.
   - Answer: Pie chart can work well.

4. Explore the relationship between advertising spending and revenue.
   - Answer: Scatter plot.

## Exercise 2: Interpret Monthly Sales

| Month | Sales (₹) |
|---|---:|
| January | 12000 |
| February | 18000 |
| March | 15000 |
| April | 25000 |
| May | 30000 |
| June | 22000 |

### Questions and Answers

1. Which month had the highest sales?
   - May: ₹30,000.

2. Which month had the lowest sales?
   - January: ₹12,000.

3. What was the difference between May and January?
   - ₹18,000.

4. Describe the trend.
   - Sales generally increased from January to May, with a decrease in March, then declined in June.

## Exercise 3: Category Sales Distribution

| Category | Sales (₹) |
|---|---:|
| Electronics | 70000 |
| Accessories | 5000 |
| Stationery | 1000 |

### Questions and Answers

1. What is total sales?
   - ₹76,000.

2. What percentage comes from Electronics?
   - Approximately 92.1%.

3. What percentage comes from Accessories?
   - Approximately 6.6%.

4. What percentage comes from Stationery?
   - Approximately 1.3%.

5. What does this distribution suggest?
   - Sales in this sample are concentrated in Electronics.
