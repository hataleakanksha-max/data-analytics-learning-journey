# Practice Exercises: Sorting and Filtering Data

## Lesson Information

- **Lesson:** 7 — Sorting and Filtering Data
- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets
- **Status:** Practiced

## Exercise 1: Sort Total Sales

### Objective

Arrange products according to their sales performance.

### Task

Sort the Total Sales column from largest to smallest.

### Expected Result

Headphones appears first with total sales of ₹24,000.

### Learning Outcome

Understand descending numerical sorting and preserve complete product records.

## Exercise 2: Filter by Category

### Objective

Display only products belonging to a selected category.

### Task

Filter Category to show Accessories.

### Expected Result

The following products remain visible:

- School Bag
- Water Bottle
- Mouse

### Learning Outcome

Understand how category filters work.

## Exercise 3: Filter by Numerical Conditions

### Task A

Filter Price to show values greater than ₹500.

**Expected result:** Two products — School Bag and Headphones.

### Task B

Filter Quantity Sold to show values less than 15.

**Expected result:** Four products — School Bag, Water Bottle, Calculator, and Headphones.

### Task C

Filter Category to show Electronics.

**Expected result:** Calculator and Headphones.

### Learning Outcome

Apply numerical and text-based conditions to a dataset.

## Exercise 4: Combine Multiple Conditions

### Task A

Filter Category to Accessories and Quantity Sold to greater than 10.

**Expected result:** Mouse.

### Task B

Identify products with Price greater than ₹100 and Quantity Sold greater than 10.

**Expected result:** Headphones and Mouse.

### Learning Outcome

Understand how multiple filters combine and why every selected condition matters.

## Exercise 5: Understand AND and OR

### Task A

Explain why a record must satisfy both conditions when using AND.

### Task B

Explain why a record can qualify when only one condition is true when using OR.

### Learning Outcome

Understand how logical conditions affect which records qualify for analysis.

## Exercise 6: Clear Filters

### Task

Apply a filter and then clear it to display the full dataset again.

### Expected Result

All eight product records become visible.

### Learning Outcome

Understand that filtering temporarily hides records rather than deleting them.

## Exercise 7: Sorting Safely

### Task

Sort the complete table by Total Sales from largest to smallest.

### Question

Why should you avoid sorting only the sales column independently of the other columns?

### Learning Outcome

Preserve the relationships between product names, categories, quantities, prices, and sales.

I learned how to organize sales records, filter categories, apply numerical conditions, combine multiple filters, and restore the complete dataset.
