# Lesson 1: Spreadsheet Fundamentals

## 1. What Is a Spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet is a digital tool used to organize, store, calculate, analyze, and visualize data.

Spreadsheets are commonly used in sales, finance, marketing, operations, and reporting.

## 2. Workbook and Worksheet

A workbook is the complete spreadsheet file.

A worksheet is an individual sheet inside a workbook.

A workbook can contain multiple worksheets.

## 3. Rows, Columns, and Cells

- **Row:** A horizontal arrangement of cells, identified by numbers.
- **Column:** A vertical arrangement of cells, identified by letters.
- **Cell:** An individual box where a row and column intersect.
- **Cell address:** A cell's location, represented by its column letter followed by its row number.

Examples:

| Cell Address | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A1 | Column A, Row 1 |
| B2 | Column B, Row 2 |
| C5 | Column C, Row 5 |

## 4. Structured Data

A structured dataset organizes information into rows and columns.

For a simple sales dataset:

- Each column represents a field, such as Product or Quantity Sold.
- Each product record occupies one row.
- The first row contains column headings.
